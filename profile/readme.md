> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This trainer disables itself in multiplayer.** Total War: WARHAMMER III has ranked multiplayer battles and co-op campaigns, and cheating in either takes something from a real opponent.
>
> The guard detects a multiplayer lobby or battle and detaches automatically. It cannot be turned off, and that is deliberate. Single-player campaigns, custom battles and Realm of Chaos or Immortal Empires against the AI only.

## What it does

A WARHAMMER III campaign is fifty hours minimum and the back half is often administration — end-turn, wait, end-turn, wait, rebuild the same province for the fourth time. Most of what this trainer does is skip the administration rather than the wars.

**Instant construction and recruitment** together remove more waiting than anything else. **Unlimited campaign movement** turns an eight-turn march into one. The battle options are there, but the campaign layer is where the real relief is.

> Faction and lord unlocks write to your campaign save. WARHAMMER III saves are large and slow to write — back up the folder before using them, and expect a longer save time afterwards.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Can I use it in multiplayer campaigns with a friend?</summary>
No. The guard detaches the trainer in any multiplayer session, co-op campaigns included. Even in a friendly co-op campaign the other player did not agree to a rewritten treasury.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work with mods?</summary>
Usually, but mods that change campaign scripting can conflict with the resource options. If something behaves oddly, test with mods disabled before opening an issue.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work with the Epic version?</summary>
Yes. Launch through the Epic client so the process starts with the right environment.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Options stopped working after a patch or DLC.</summary>
WARHAMMER III patches frequently and DLC changes campaign structures. Check the Releases page for a build matching your game version.
</details>
